Features

Not a search box. A hive mind that connects the dots.

Hivemind models people, projects, systems, documents and decisions as a graph — so it can follow the relationships a keyword search can't.

01 — ASK

Answers with receipts.

Ask anything in plain language. Every claim links to the ticket, document or message it came from, and the graph shows the path.

02 — EXPERTISE

Who actually knows.

Expertise from evidence — what people wrote, fixed and were thanked for — not the org chart.

Why it matters

Knowledge leaves with people, hides in threads and repeats in meetings. Hivemind keeps it.

  • New hires find the answer — and the person — without asking around
  • Decisions stop being re-argued: what, who, when and why are on record
  • Leaders see knowledge risks before someone goes on leave
