How it works
01

Everything is connected

People, teams, projects, tools, docs, tickets and chats become one map. Lines show who wrote what, who owns what, and which meeting a decision came from.

02

Ask in plain words

The brain follows the connections to the answer, like asking around the office, only instantly.

03

See the proof, stay current

Every answer links to the original source. When a new message changes a decision, the old one is marked outdated automatically.

Questions it answers
"Why did we choose Postgres?"The decision, who made it, and the meeting and doc it came from.
"Who should I ask about Kafka?"The person who actually works with it, not just whoever owns it on paper.
"Why is checkout blocked?"The blocking ticket, its status, and who can unblock it.
"What changed about login?"The full decision history, with the outdated choices marked.
