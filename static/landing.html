Product

Answers your team can verify.

Every answer and every generated document is grounded in graph queries and links back to the original source.

Cited answers

Ask in plain language. Answers reference the documents, tickets and messages they come from, and the graph shows the path.

Real expertise

Find who actually knows a system from what they wrote, fixed and were credited for — alongside formal ownership.

Decision history

Trace every decision to the meeting or thread where it was made. Replaced decisions are marked outdated automatically.

PRDs and sprint plans

Generate documents from live project data: blockers first, owners from assignments and expertise, risks flagged.

Knowledge risks

Graph algorithms surface key connectors, single points of failure, stalled work and knowledge no one is linked to.

Private workspaces

Each company gets its own graph and access key. Agent queries are read-only, and connection secrets are never exposed.

Ask

Know who to ask, and why things are the way they are.

The agent follows real relationships — who wrote what, who fixed what, which meeting made which call — instead of guessing.

  • Every claim cited to a document, ticket or message
  • The graph highlights exactly what the answer used
  • Also available in Slack with /brain
Plan

Sprint plans and PRDs that start from facts.

Pick a project. Company Brain gathers its decisions, open tickets, blockers and experts, and writes a plan your team can act on.

  • Blockers scheduled before the work they block
  • Owners from assignments or hands-on expertise
  • Export to Markdown or PDF, or post to a channel
Insights

See knowledge risks before they become incidents.

FalkorDB graph algorithms reveal who connects the organisation, where one person holds all the knowledge, and which work has stalled.

  • Key connectors via betweenness centrality
  • Single-expert systems and stalled tickets
  • Central topics via PageRank
Why it matters

Less searching. Fewer repeated questions. Faster onboarding.

New engineers shouldn't have to ask the same five people the same questions. The answer, the decision behind it and the person who knows should be one search away.

  • Onboard faster with cited answers instead of tribal knowledge
  • Stop re-litigating decisions — see what was decided, by whom, and why
  • Spot single points of failure before someone goes on leave

How it works

From scattered tools to one graph in minutes.

No migration. Connect the tools you already use, or paste and upload what you have.

1

Connect

Slack, Jira, Linear and GitHub, or upload documents, CSV and JSON. People, projects and systems are linked automatically.

2

Ask

The agent queries the graph with full-text search, multi-hop Cypher and graph algorithms, and shows each step live.

3

Act

Generate PRDs and sprint plans, and share answers and decisions to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord or Google Chat.
