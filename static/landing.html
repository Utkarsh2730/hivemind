Product

Answers your team can verify.

Every answer and every generated document is grounded in graph queries and links back to the original source.

Cited answers

Ask in plain language. Answers reference the documents, tickets and messages they come from, and the graph shows the path.

Real expertise

Find who actually knows a system from what they wrote, fixed and were credited for, alongside formal ownership.

Decision history

Trace every decision to the meeting or thread where it was made. Replaced decisions are marked outdated automatically.

PRDs and sprint plans

Generate documents from live project data: blockers first, owners from assignments and expertise, risks flagged.

Knowledge risks

Graph algorithms surface key connectors, single points of failure, stalled work and knowledge no one is linked to.

Isolated workspaces

Each company gets its own graph and access key. Agent queries are read-only, and connection secrets are never exposed.

How it works

From scattered tools to one graph in minutes.

No migration. Connect the tools you already use, or paste and upload what you have.

01

Connect

Slack, Jira, Linear and GitHub, or upload documents, CSV and JSON. People, projects and systems are linked automatically.

02

Ask

The agent queries the graph with full-text search, multi-hop Cypher and graph algorithms, and shows each step.

03

Act

Generate PRDs and sprint plans, and share answers and decisions to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord or Google Chat.
