Manage your company data

Tell the brain who works here and what you build. Put one item per line and separate fields with |. You can add more later; existing items are updated, not duplicated.
Paste anything your company knows: a doc, meeting notes, a ticket or a chat message. The brain links it to the right people, projects and tools. If it records a decision that replaces an older one, the older one is marked outdated.
Upload many items at once:
• .txt / .md: each file becomes a doc, titled with the file name
• .csv: one row per item, with columns kind,title,text,author,date,channel,status,assignee
• .json: a list of items with those fields, or {"people":[], "projects":[], "systems":[], "sources":[]}
Older items are processed first, so newer decisions replace older ones.
